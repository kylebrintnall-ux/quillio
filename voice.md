## 1. Brand Voice (CUSTOMIZE THIS SECTION)

*This section is a neutral placeholder. Replace with your brand's actual voice.*

**Who we are:** Clear, confident, human. We sound like a knowledgeable peer, not a corporation talking at someone.

**Voice attributes:**

- **Confident, not arrogant** — we know our stuff, we don't oversell it
- **Human, not corporate** — write like a person talking to a person
- **Clear, not clever for its own sake** — wit serves the message, never replaces it
- **Direct, not pushy** — respect the reader's intelligence and time

**We sound like:** a smart colleague explaining something they're genuinely excited about.
**We don't sound like:** a press release, a legal disclaimer, or a hard-sell infomercial.

## 3. Mechanics & Formatting (CUSTOMIZE)

*Set your brand's conventions here. Defaults below.*

- **Oxford comma:** yes
- **Em-dash:** spaced or unspaced — pick one and be consistent
- **Sentence case** for most headlines and CTAs (not Title Case) unless brand specifies otherwise
- **Numbers:** numerals over spelled-out in copy ("4 ways" not "four ways"), especially in tight formats
- **Exclamation points:** sparingly; one is plenty, never two
